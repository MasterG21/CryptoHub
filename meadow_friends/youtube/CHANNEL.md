## Channel description

Paste this into Settings → Channel → Basic info → Description. YouTube allows
1,000 characters; this is about 830, which leaves room to add an upload day and
a contact line.

Only the first ~150 characters show in search results and channel previews, so
the opening sentence is written to stand on its own.

---

Milo Meets is a gentle 3D cartoon for toddlers. In every episode Milo the mouse
meets a new animal friend, they have a little chat, and your child is invited to
make the sound out loud.

Made for ages 1 to 4. Every episode is short, calm and colourful — no loud
noises, no fast cutting, no scary surprises. Just friendly characters, simple
conversations, and real pauses so little ones have time to join in.

What your child practises:
🐮 Animal names and the sounds they make
🗣️ Speaking, listening and taking turns
📖 Early reading — every line is on screen with clear English subtitles
💛 Kindness, curiosity and saying hello

New episode every Friday. Subscribe and come and meet a new friend with Milo.

---

### Notes on the copy

- **It talks to parents, not children.** Nobody under four reads a channel
  description; the person deciding whether to press play does.
- **No "tell us in the comments".** Comments are disabled on made-for-kids
  content, so a comment call-to-action is a dead end that makes the channel look
  like it does not know its own platform.
- **The keywords are in sentences, not a pile.** "Animal sounds", "toddlers",
  "subtitles" and "3D cartoon" all appear naturally. A keyword list at the
  bottom reads as spam to parents and buys very little in search.
- **Swap the upload day** for whatever you can actually sustain. A stated
  schedule you miss is worse than no schedule.
- **Add a business email** under Settings → Channel → Advanced if you ever want
  brand contact; it lives in the About tab, not the description.

## Episode titles

The channel name is the format, so let the titles complete it:

    Milo Meets the Cow 🐄 Animal Sounds for Toddlers
    Milo Meets the Duck 🦆 Animal Sounds for Toddlers
    Milo Meets the Lion 🦁 Animal Sounds for Toddlers

The first episode covers several animals at once, so:

    Milo Meets His Meadow Friends 🐘 Animal Sounds Story for Toddlers

## Series naming

The show inside the video is still called *Meadow Friends*, which works fine as
a series name under a *Milo Meets* channel — a channel brand and a series title
are allowed to differ. If you would rather they match, the in-video title card
is rendered from `script.json` (`meta.title`), and only the opening scene needs
re-rendering, not the whole episode.
