## What to know about monetising this

Check the current thresholds in YouTube Studio before planning — YouTube
changes them.

1. **Made-for-kids videos earn differently.** Personalised ads are off on MFK
   content, so it runs contextual ads only, which typically pay a lower RPM.
   Comments are disabled, and so are cards, end screens, Super Chat and channel
   memberships. Plan the channel around watch time, not end-screen funnels.
2. **You need to be in the YouTube Partner Program first.** The main bar is
   1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or
   10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days, along with an AdSense
   account, 2-step verification, no active Community Guidelines strikes, and
   residence in a country where YPP runs. A lower tier (500 subscribers, 3
   public uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch hours or 3 million Shorts views)
   unlocks fan funding but not ad revenue.
3. **Repetitive content is not monetisable.** YouTube's inauthentic-content
   policy excludes mass-produced and templated uploads. Re-running this project
   with the animals swapped is exactly that pattern — write a new story, and
   vary the staging and the songs, rather than reskinning one render.
4. **Nothing here can be claimed.** Characters and sets are built in code, the
   voices are synthesised from an MIT-licensed model, and the music and animal
   noises are generated in `render/voices.py` and `render/make_audio.py`. No
   stock assets, samples or loops anywhere in the video.
