Before you start

1A crypto wallet on your phone — Trust Wallet, MetaMask or Binance Wallet.
2A small amount of BNB in it for network fees. A few dollars covers this.
3About 15 minutes, somewhere you won't be rushed.

Never share your private key or seed phrase

Not with me, not with any AI, not with anyone who offers to launch your token for you. A seed phrase is your entire wallet. Anyone who has it can empty it, instantly and permanently, and no one can reverse it.

A real launchpad never asks for it. It asks you to connect your wallet and then to approve each action inside your own wallet app. That is the only thing you should ever be doing.

The steps

  1. Save the logo to your device

    You'll upload this in step 5. On a phone you can also press and hold the image at the top of this page and choose Save Image.

    512 × 512 PNG — inside brew.family's 5 MB limit.

  2. Put some BNB in your wallet

    Creating a token costs a network fee, paid in BNB. Buy a small amount on an exchange and send it to your wallet address, or buy directly inside the wallet app.

    Start with only what you're willing to spend on this. You can always add more.

  3. Open brew.family and connect

    Tap Connect wallet at the top right. Your wallet app will ask you to approve the connection — that's normal, and it gives the site no power to move your funds.

  4. Tap Create

    The + button in the bar at the bottom of the screen. You'll land on 01 Make it yours.

  5. Fill in “Make it yours”

    Upload the logo where it says Add token image, then copy each field below into the matching box.

    Keep that last paragraph. It costs you nothing, and leaving it out is what turns an ordinary token into a promise you didn't mean to make.

  6. Choose your pair on “Set your launch”

    The pair is what your token trades against — the thing people spend to buy CPU.

    Pick WBNB or a stablecoin. They're what almost everyone on BNB Chain already holds, so buying your token is one tap for them.

    About pairing with a Micron stock token. A pair is not a reward — it only sets what CPU is priced in. Pairing against a thinly traded asset means anyone who wants CPU must first go and buy that, which is a step most people simply won't take. It's the most common way a launch quietly dies.

  7. Read “Review & create” slowly

    Check the name, the ticker and the image. This is the last screen before it's real — a token can't be renamed after it's created.

  8. Approve in your wallet

    Your wallet app will pop up asking you to confirm and pay the fee. Read what it says, then approve. After a few seconds your token exists.

  9. Copy your contract address and keep it safe

    This long 0x… address is how anyone finds your token. Paste it somewhere you won't lose it — it's the single most important thing you now own.

    Look it up on BscScan to confirm it's really there.

Once it's live

·Don't sell into your own holders. People watch the creator's wallet, and it's the fastest way to lose the trust you just built.
·Liquidity is what lets people sell. If buyers can't get out, they won't get in. Locking it is the normal signal that you don't intend to pull it.
·Nothing here makes a token succeed. This removes the reasons people bounce off in the first ten seconds. Attention is a separate job, and the hard one.

Leave this for later

The part that pays holders in Micron stock tokens is built and tested, but it isn't a step you can click through — and paying holders in a tokenized stock is very probably a regulated securities offering. That needs a lawyer before it needs a developer.

Launch the token first. It stands on its own.
