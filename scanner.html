Robinhood Chain Token Scanner

Screens tokens for structural rug risk. Runs entirely in your browser.

1
Open the launchpad page in another tab and press Ctrl+S (Windows) or Cmd+S (Mac) to save it. Choose "Webpage, Complete" or "Webpage, HTML Only".
2
Drag that saved file into the box below. Repeat for as many pages as you like.
3
Press Scan. Takes a few seconds per token.

What this tells you. It checks whether a contract can be used against holders — hidden mint functions, admin controls, supply concentrated in one wallet, a deployer who has launched many tokens before. Those are verifiable facts.

What it does not tell you. Whether the price will go up. Nothing here predicts returns. A token can pass every check and still go to zero. Use this to avoid traps, not to pick winners.
