## What to know about monetising this

These are the platform rules that actually bear on this video. Check the current
thresholds in YouTube Studio before planning — YouTube changes them.

1. **Made-for-kids videos earn differently.** Personalised ads are turned off on
   MFK content, so it runs contextual ads only, which typically pay a lower RPM.
   Comments are disabled, and so are cards, end screens, Super Chat, channel
   memberships and Save-to-playlist. Plan the channel around watch time rather
   than around end-screen funnels.
2. **You need to be in the YouTube Partner Program first.** The main
   monetisation bar is 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 valid public watch
   hours in 12 months or 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days, along
   with an AdSense account, 2-step verification, no active Community Guidelines
   strikes, and residence in a country where YPP is available. A lower tier
   (500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, 3,000 watch hours or 3 million
   Shorts views) unlocks fan funding but not ad revenue.
3. **Repetitive content is not monetisable.** YouTube's inauthentic-content
   policy excludes mass-produced and templated uploads. Re-rendering this
   project with a different animal list is exactly the pattern that policy is
   aimed at, so vary the songs, the art and the format between uploads rather
   than shipping the same video with swapped nouns.
4. **Nothing here will get a Content ID claim.** The artwork and animation are
   drawn in code in `web/`, the music and every animal sound are synthesised
   from scratch in `render/make_audio.py`, and the two fonts are SIL OFL, which
   permits commercial use. There are no stock assets, samples or third-party
   loops anywhere in the video.
