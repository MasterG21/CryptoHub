Animal Sounds Song - Sing Along with the Animal Friends

Sing along and make the animal sounds! Meet eight friendly animals - the cow, the duck, the cat, the dog, the frog, the lion, the monkey and the elephant - and copy each sound out loud. Then play a guessing game and sing the animal song together at the end.

Big, colourful animations and clear English subtitles on every line, so little ones can listen, watch and read along.
